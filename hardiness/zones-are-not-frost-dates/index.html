Hardiness zones are not frost dates

Two different questions: will this plant survive the winter, and when can I plant it out?

Two questions, two measurements

A hardiness zone answers: how cold does the worst night of a typical winter get here? It is built from the average of each year's lowest winter temperature. It is useful for choosing perennial plants (trees, shrubs and flowers that must live through the winter).

A frost date answers: when does the last freezing night usually come in spring, and when does the first one come in autumn? Those dates decide when tender vegetables and annual flowers can safely go out, and how long the growing season is. They are a different measurement and come from different weather statistics.

Why the same zone can have different frost dates

Two places can have the same coldest-night average and still differ in how long their cold season lasts. A coastal garden may rarely get a hard freeze but has frost for a long stretch; an inland garden may have a short season and a very cold winter minimum. Altitude, nearness to water, urban heat and the shape of the land all change the dates.

What this site shows, and what it does not

This site gives the USDA hardiness zone for each ZIP code, from the 2023 map. It does not publish frost dates, because the data we would need were not read from an official source for this site. For frost dates in your area, ask your state's cooperative extension service or look at your local weather service's climate information.

The USDA's own caution

The USDA states that the map is based on average lowest winter temperatures, a general guide for perennials, and that wind, soil, moisture, humidity, snow, winter sunshine, heat and the way a plant is placed and planted all affect survival. A zone number is only one of the inputs when you decide what and when to plant.

Source: USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map, "How to Use the Maps" (planthardiness.ars.usda.gov), read on 13 November 2026.

Is my last frost date the same as the zone?

No. The zone describes the winter minimum; the frost date describes the end of the frost season.

Can I plant when the zone says so?

The zone does not give a planting date. Use your local last frost date.
