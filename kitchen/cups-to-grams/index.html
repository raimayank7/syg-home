How to fill a cup so the weight is right

  • Flour, besan, cocoa: stir the flour, spoon it into the cup until heaped, then level with the back of a knife. Do not tap or press.
  • Sugar, rice, dal, sooji: scoop and level. These do not pack much.
  • Brown sugar: press it into the cup. Recipes mean a packed cup.
  • Ghee, butter: soften first, or measure by weight. A cold lump leaves air gaps.
  • Liquids: use a clear cup on a flat surface and read at eye level.

Questions people ask

How many grams is 1 cup of atta?

About 120 g when the atta is spooned into a 240 ml cup and levelled. If you scoop the cup straight into the bag, the atta packs down and the cup can hold 140 g or more.

How many grams is 1 cup of sugar?

About 200 g of ordinary granulated sugar. Powdered sugar is much lighter, about 120 g a cup.

Is an Indian cup 200 ml, 240 ml or 250 ml?

There is no single Indian cup. Measuring cup sets sold in India are usually 240 ml or 250 ml. Tea cups and katoris are smaller. This page uses 240 ml. For a 250 ml cup, add about 4 percent.

Why do my grams not match the table?

Powders settle. Flour that is sifted, spooned or scooped can differ by 20 percent in the same cup. For baking, a kitchen scale is the only exact way.
