About the sources and what is missing

Every official figure here comes from two documents. This page also says what is missing.

Documents read on 13 November 2026

  1. Room air conditioner notifications: the consolidated PDF linked as "Room Air Conditioners" on the Bureau of Energy Efficiency's Standards and Labelling page (beeindia.gov.in), containing S.O. 2528(E) of 8 August 2017 and the amendments S.O. 3897(E) of 29 October 2019, S.O. 4485(E) of 10 December 2020 and S.O. 4634(E) of 10 November 2021; plus the two later amendments from the Gazette of India (egazette.gov.in, search by Ministry of Power): S.O. 2794(E) of 16 July 2024 and S.O. 3984(E) of 28 August 2025. It supplies the ISEER definition, the test method (1,600 cooling hours, 24 to 43 degrees Celsius), the printed star bands, the review every two years and the 24 degree default rule.
  2. BEE Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026 (Gazette of India, 26 December 2025), from beestarlabel.com: label particulars, the list of mandatory appliances (room air conditioners are in Schedule A) and the note that one ton of refrigeration is 3,517 watts.

What was not found

  • An official cooling load per square metre. None exists in the documents read, so the size calculator uses your own assumption.
  • A saving figure for the 24 degree setting. The notification gives none.
  • Whether a later amendment than S.O. 3984(E) exists. None was seen in the Ministry of Power's Gazette listing for September 2025; check the Gazette for anything newer.

What this site adds

Arithmetic and plain explanations. This site is independent and is not connected with BEE or the Ministry of Power.

Sources, read on 13 November 2026: Ministry of Power / Bureau of Energy Efficiency notifications on room air conditioners: S.O. 2528(E) of 8 August 2017 and its amendments S.O. 3897(E) of 29 October 2019, S.O. 4485(E) of 10 December 2020 and S.O. 4634(E) of 10 November 2021 (consolidated text on beeindia.gov.in), S.O. 2794(E) of 16 July 2024 (Gazette of India, 18 July 2024, CG-DL-E-18072024-255521) and S.O. 3984(E) of 28 August 2025 (Gazette of India No. 3879, 1 September 2025, CG-DL-E-02092025-265874), both from egazette.gov.in; and the BEE Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026 (Gazette of India, 26 December 2025), published on beestarlabel.com.
