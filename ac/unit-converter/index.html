The conversions

  • Ton of refrigeration (TR): 3,517 watts, as stated by BEE in the label specification for room air conditioners (Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026). BEE also says the calculated tonnage on a label is rounded to two decimal places.
  • Kilowatt: 1,000 watts.
  • Btu per hour: 1 Btu per hour is 0.29307107 watts (the standard definition of the British thermal unit), so 3,517 watts is about 12,000 Btu per hour. The "ton" of the market is therefore often described as 12,000 Btu/h.

What the label says

A BEE label for a room air conditioner shows the rated capacity of the model; the regulation lists the particulars on a label as the appliance name, brand, model number, energy performance parameter, label series, rated capacity, star level, country of origin and the BEE logo. Compare units by watts or tons of rated capacity, not by the name in an advertisement.

Is a 1.5 ton AC exactly 1.5 TR?

The model name is a market convention; the label shows the rated capacity. Check it.

Are Btu per hour official?

The Btu is a standard unit; BEE's own definition uses watts and tons of refrigeration.
