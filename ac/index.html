AC size calculator: tons from room area

Enter the floor area and your own cooling assumption. The page converts the load to watts, tons of refrigeration and Btu per hour.

What this calculator does

It works out a cooling load and converts it to the units air conditioners are sold in. The conversion is official: the BEE label specification states that one ton of refrigeration is equivalent to 3,517 watts (Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026, Schedule C, label note for room air conditioners). The load itself is your assumption: you enter a number of watts of cooling per square metre of floor and, if you wish, extra watts for heat sources such as a kitchen or several computers.

Why the watts per square metre is a box and not a fact

No official figure gives the cooling needed per square metre for every room: it depends on the climate, the top floor or not, the sun on the walls and windows, the insulation, the people and the appliances. We did not find an official number, so the default of 150 W per square metre is only a starting assumption for you to change, and the answer is a rough starting point. Have an installer measure the room and calculate the load, and follow the unit's rated capacity, which BEE requires to be shown on the label.

Reading the result

The tons figure tells you roughly what rated capacity to look for. Labels also show capacity in watts. A unit that is much too small will not cool the room; a unit that is much too large may be wasteful. The ISEER on the label tells you how efficiently it uses electricity: see the running cost calculator.

Sources, read on 13 November 2026: Ministry of Power / Bureau of Energy Efficiency notifications on room air conditioners: S.O. 2528(E) of 8 August 2017 and its amendments S.O. 3897(E) of 29 October 2019, S.O. 4485(E) of 10 December 2020 and S.O. 4634(E) of 10 November 2021 (consolidated text on beeindia.gov.in), S.O. 2794(E) of 16 July 2024 (Gazette of India, 18 July 2024, CG-DL-E-18072024-255521) and S.O. 3984(E) of 28 August 2025 (Gazette of India No. 3879, 1 September 2025, CG-DL-E-02092025-265874), both from egazette.gov.in; and the BEE Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026 (Gazette of India, 26 December 2025), published on beestarlabel.com.

Why do you say tons if the label shows watts?

Both are used. The conversion between them is official: one ton of refrigeration is 3,517 W.

Is 150 W per square metre right for my room?

It may not be. It is an assumption you can change, not an official value.
