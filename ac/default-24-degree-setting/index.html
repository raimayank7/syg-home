The 24 degree default setting for room ACs

One short official rule affects every labelled room air conditioner sold in India.

The rule

The Ministry of Power notification S.O. 3897(E) of 29 October 2019 (an amendment of the 2017 star labelling notification) says that all brands and types of star-labelled room air conditioners, namely multi-stage capacity air conditioners, unitary air conditioners and split air conditioners, rated from one star to five star and up to a rated cooling capacity of 10,465 watts (9,000 kcal per hour), and manufactured, commercially purchased or sold in India, shall ensure default setting of temperature in the room air conditioners at twenty-four degrees Celsius with effect from 1 January 2020. The same amendment added a line to the application form, "Default temperature setting of the room air conditioner at 24 degree celsius? Yes/No".

What it means for you

It is a rule about what the unit does by default: it is set to 24 degrees when it leaves the factory or when reset, and you can change it with the remote. The notification says nothing about how much electricity a different setting saves, so this site gives no saving figure.

Capacity limit

The rule applies up to 10,465 watts of rated cooling capacity, which is about 2.98 tons of refrigeration at 3,517 watts per ton (10,465 / 3,517). Larger units are outside this notification.

Sources, read on 13 November 2026: Ministry of Power / Bureau of Energy Efficiency notifications on room air conditioners: S.O. 2528(E) of 8 August 2017 and its amendments S.O. 3897(E) of 29 October 2019, S.O. 4485(E) of 10 December 2020 and S.O. 4634(E) of 10 November 2021 (consolidated text on beeindia.gov.in), S.O. 2794(E) of 16 July 2024 (Gazette of India, 18 July 2024, CG-DL-E-18072024-255521) and S.O. 3984(E) of 28 August 2025 (Gazette of India No. 3879, 1 September 2025, CG-DL-E-02092025-265874), both from egazette.gov.in; and the BEE Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026 (Gazette of India, 26 December 2025), published on beestarlabel.com.

Can I set a different temperature?

The notification governs the default setting, not what you choose afterwards.

Does the rule apply to old units?

It applies from 1 January 2020 to units manufactured, purchased or sold from then, as the notification says.
