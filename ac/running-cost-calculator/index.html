AC running cost and ISEER comparison

Enter the cooling capacity, hours and load, then the ISEER from the label of one or two units, and your electricity rate.

What ISEER means

BEE defines the Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio (ISEER) as the ratio of the total annual amount of heat the equipment may remove from the indoor air when operated for cooling to the total annual amount of energy consumed by the equipment during the same period (room air conditioner notification, definitions). Both are in kilowatt-hours, so ISEER is kWh of cooling per kWh of electricity. A higher ISEER means less electricity for the same cooling.

The calculation

Electricity (kWh) = cooling delivered (kWh) / ISEER. Cooling delivered = capacity (kW) x hours x average load (%). Example: a 1.5 TR unit (5,275.5 W) for 1,600 hours at 50 per cent load delivers 5.2755 x 1600 x 0.5 = 4,220 kWh of cooling; at an ISEER of 4.0 it uses 1,055 kWh, and at an ISEER of 5.0 it uses 844 kWh. The 1,600 hours is the number of cooling hours per year that BEE's test method assumes for rating (the notification states a bin temperature range of 24 to 43 degrees Celsius and 1,600 cooling hours per annum); your use will differ, so the hours are a box you can change.

Limits

This is a simple division, not a prediction. ISEER is measured under the test conditions in the BEE notification and applies to a whole season; your weather, settings, room and maintenance change the result. The load percentage is your assumption. The star bands for each period are on the star label page; enter the ISEER shown on the unit's label.

Sources, read on 13 November 2026: Ministry of Power / Bureau of Energy Efficiency notifications on room air conditioners: S.O. 2528(E) of 8 August 2017 and its amendments S.O. 3897(E) of 29 October 2019, S.O. 4485(E) of 10 December 2020 and S.O. 4634(E) of 10 November 2021 (consolidated text on beeindia.gov.in), S.O. 2794(E) of 16 July 2024 (Gazette of India, 18 July 2024, CG-DL-E-18072024-255521) and S.O. 3984(E) of 28 August 2025 (Gazette of India No. 3879, 1 September 2025, CG-DL-E-02092025-265874), both from egazette.gov.in; and the BEE Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations, 2026 (Gazette of India, 26 December 2025), published on beestarlabel.com.

Where do I find ISEER?

On the BEE label of the unit: the regulation lists the energy performance parameter as one of the particulars on the label.

Does the calculator know my star level?

No. It uses the ISEER number you type.
