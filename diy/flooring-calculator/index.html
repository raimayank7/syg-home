Flooring calculator

Enter the room size and the area one pack covers. The page adds waste and tells you how many packs to buy and how much will be left over.

How to use it

Plank flooring such as laminate, vinyl planks and engineered wood is sold by the pack, and each pack states the area it covers, for example 2.2 square metres or 22 square feet. That is the number to type into the pack box. The calculator multiplies your floor area by one plus the waste percentage, divides by the pack coverage and rounds up to whole packs. It also shows how much extra floor you end up buying, which is useful for deciding whether to keep it for repairs.

Ten per cent is a good allowance for a room laid straight along its length. Plank floors waste material at the end of every row, where the last plank is cut to fit; if the offcut is long enough it starts the next row, but some are too short. A diagonal layout or herringbone pattern wastes more, so use 15 to 20 per cent. A room with several corners, an L shape or a staircase landing also needs more.

Worked example

A living room 5 m by 4 m is 20 square metres. Packs cover 2.2 square metres each, and we allow 10 per cent waste.

  • 20 x 1.10 = 22 square metres needed.
  • 22 / 2.2 = 10.0, so 10 packs, which is exactly 22 square metres.
  • Spare floor: 22 - 20 = 2 square metres, enough for about one pack of repairs.

If the room had needed 22.01 square metres you would buy 11 packs, since a pack cannot be split. That is why the page rounds up.

What to measure

Measure the walls at the floor, not above the skirting, and treat alcoves, bay windows and door openings as part of the rectangle if the floor runs through them. Subtract only large permanent obstacles such as a built-in cupboard base. See measuring a room for a method that avoids the usual mistakes. If one pack is the whole difference between finishing in a day and waiting for a delivery, buy the extra pack: it is cheaper than a shortage.

Before you lay it

Floating floors are normally left in the room for a day or two before laying so they match its temperature and humidity; the maker's leaflet gives the exact period. The sub-floor should be dry, flat and clean, and a gap is left along the walls because the boards expand a little. Read the maker's instructions, which override any general rule on this page.

Does the calculator include underlay?

No. Underlay is sold in rolls with its own coverage, so use the same area and the roll's coverage to count rolls, with a small allowance for overlaps.

Should I buy for the cupboard area or under appliances?

Floor under fitted units that will never move is usually left out. Floor under a washing machine or fridge is normally laid, since they get replaced.

Why is my answer higher than area divided by pack size?

Because of the waste percentage and rounding up to whole packs.

Estimates only. Real jobs differ with the surface, the product and the skill of the worker. Read the maker's instructions on the tin, bag or box, and order a little extra.
