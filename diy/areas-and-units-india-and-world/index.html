Areas and units: India and the world

The same flat can be quoted at three different sizes. Here is how to compare them and what to use for materials.

Units that are the same everywhere

The square metre is the standard unit and equals 10.764 square feet. A square foot is 0.0929 square metres. A square yard is 9 square feet, which in north India is a gaj. An acre is 4,046.86 square metres (43,560 square feet) and a hectare is 10,000 square metres. These do not change from place to place, and the area converter uses them.

Units that are not the same everywhere

Land in India is also measured in bigha, biswa, kanal, marla, cent, guntha and others. Their sizes depend on the state, and sometimes on the district, and some have been redefined by state laws. A marla in one state is not a marla in another. There is no single answer that is safe, so this site does not convert them. When you need to compare, take the area in square feet or square metres from the sale deed or the land record, which states it.

Flats: three areas

  • Carpet area: the floor inside the walls that you could cover with a carpet, including the area of kitchen and bathrooms, and not including the thickness of the walls.
  • Built-up area: carpet area plus the thickness of the walls and the balconies and terraces of the unit.
  • Super built-up area (or saleable area): built-up area plus a share of the common areas: stairs, lift, lobby, club house.

The difference between carpet and super built-up can be large, so the same flat can have very different figures. Ask for the carpet area in writing and compare flats on it.

What to use for materials

For flooring, tiles, paint and wallpaper, measure the actual rooms. A brochure area includes walls and common space that you do not floor. The guide to measuring a room shows how. For an estimate of the total floor to be covered in a flat, carpet area is a good starting point, less the bathrooms if they are tiled separately and less the parts you will leave bare.

Worked comparison

A plot of 100 sq m is 1,076 sq ft. In gaj it is 119.6 gaj. In acres it is 0.0247 acres. A plot described as 200 gaj has 1,800 sq ft and 167.2 sq m. Put your own numbers into the converter and check each against the paper.

Other countries

In the US and the UK, floor areas are normally in square feet, though the UK uses square metres in new documents. Europe and most of the world use square metres. Building materials follow the market: tiles in the UK and Europe are in millimetres, in the US in inches.

Which size should I pay for?

Compare carpet area, and ask for it in writing.

Why are there so many local land units?

They come from historical measures, such as the area one plough could turn in a day, and were never unified.
