DIY guides

The reasoning behind the calculators, in plain words.

These guides explain the reasoning behind the numbers: how to measure a room properly, why paint covers less than the tin says, how much tile waste to allow, what a concrete mix means, how bricks and mortar are counted and how areas are quoted. Each one links to the calculator that uses it, so you can read the reason first and then put in your own room.

Start with how to measure a room: a wrong measurement ruins every estimate that follows. Then pick the job: decorating (paint, wallpaper), floors and walls (tiles, flooring), or building (concrete, bricks, plaster, gravel and soil). All of them are general practice, not a replacement for the instructions on the product or an engineer's drawing.
