Brick calculator

Enter the wall size, the openings and the brick size. You get the number of bricks with breakage, and the cement and sand for the mortar.

Counting bricks in a wall

Each brick in a wall occupies its own size plus half a mortar joint on every side. A modular brick of 190 x 90 x 90 mm with 10 mm joints takes up a face of 200 mm x 100 mm, which is 0.02 square metres. One square metre of wall therefore needs 1 / 0.02 = 50 bricks when the wall is one brick wide (a half-brick wall, or 90 mm thick, laid with the long face showing), and 100 bricks when it is two bricks thick (a wall as thick as one brick is long, 190 mm).

If the wall has doors or windows, give their combined area in the openings box and the page subtracts it.

Mortar

The mortar is the wall volume minus the volume of the bricks themselves. For the single wall that is 2.7 m3 of wall less 1,500 x 0.19 x 0.09 x 0.09 = 2.31 m3 of brick, which leaves 0.39 m3 of wet mortar. Dry cement and sand take about 1.27 times that (0.50 m3), shared out by the mix. In a 1:6 mortar that is one part cement to six of sand: 3 bags of cement and 0.43 m3 of sand for the whole 10 m wall. Plain mortar for brickwork is often 1:4 to 1:6; the stronger the mix, the more cement. Your engineer or mason will tell you which one the job needs.

Other brick sizes

Choose a preset in the size box: the Indian modular brick (190 x 90 x 90), the older traditional Indian size (230 x 110 x 70), and a typical US modular brick (about 194 x 92 x 57 mm). Sizes vary between kilns, so measure a few bricks from your supplier and take the average. A difference of 5 mm in length changes the count by a few per cent over a big wall.

Checks before you order

Bricks arrive with some broken in transport, so 5 per cent is the minimum allowance; hand-moulded bricks may need more. Order from one batch for a uniform colour. Wall thickness is also set by the load and the building rules, not by cost, so confirm it with the drawing. For the pattern of bricks and what it means for the count, see brick bond and mortar.

Does this work for AAC blocks or concrete blocks?

Yes: choose "My own size" in the size box and type the block length, width and height in millimetres. Thin-bed AAC blocks use a much thinner joint (2 to 3 mm), so also change the joint box.

What is a half-brick wall?

A wall one brick wide, usually used for partitions. A "one-brick" wall is as thick as one brick is long and counts twice the bricks per square metre.

How do I count bricks for a curved wall?

Measure the length along the curve (with a tape or string) and use that as the wall length.
