The units on this page

Building quantities mix metric and older units, and the same area is quoted differently by a shop, an architect and a land record. This page converts between the units that are exact and settled:

  • Square metre (m2): the metric unit of area.
  • Square foot (sq ft): 0.09290304 square metres, exactly. Flats and rooms in India are advertised in square feet.
  • Square yard: 9 square feet, 0.83612736 square metres. In north India a square yard is called a gaj, and plots are sold by the gaj.
  • Acre: 4,046.8564 square metres, or 43,560 square feet.
  • Hectare: 10,000 square metres.
  • Square inch and square centimetre: for small things such as tiles.

Units such as bigha, biswa, kanal, marla, cent and guntha are *not* converted on this page, because their sizes depend on the state or even the district, and some have been redefined by local law. For land, use the figure written in the government record for the place.

A room of 12 ft by 10 ft is 120 sq ft, or 11.15 m2. A plot of 200 gaj is 1,800 sq ft, which is 167.2 m2.

Carpet area, built-up area and super built-up area

Flats are sold with different area figures, and the same flat can look 20 to 30 per cent larger on paper depending on which one is quoted. Carpet area is the floor you can lay a carpet on inside the walls. Built-up area adds the thickness of walls. Super built-up area adds a share of lifts, stairs and common areas. For buying floor material or paint, only the carpet area counts, and you should measure the rooms yourself rather than use the brochure. See measuring a room.

How many square feet are in a gaj?

One gaj (square yard) is 9 square feet.

How do I convert square feet to cubic feet?

You cannot convert an area to a volume without a depth. Multiply the area in square feet by the depth in feet.

Is one square metre about ten square feet?

It is 10.76 square feet, so ten is close enough for a quick check.
