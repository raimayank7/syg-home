Cooking converter: cups to grams

Pick an ingredient, because a cup of flour is not a cup of honey.

A cup of flour and a cup of honey are the same volume and very different weights, so a cup-to-gram converter has to know which ingredient you mean. Choose the ingredient above, type the amount, pick the unit and the converter shows grams, ounces, cups and spoons together. There is a page for each of 124 ingredients, with the full table of fractions of a cup, the weight of 50 g to 1 kg in cups and spoons, and a measuring note.

The weights are household-measure weights from the US Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central (public domain). We have not copied them from recipe sites, and where a figure had to be worked out from a spoon weight the page says so. For baking, weigh. For everyday cooking, the cup and spoon figures here are close enough.
