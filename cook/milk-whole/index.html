Milk, whole: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of milk weighs about 244 g (8.6 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Milk, whole, 3.25% milkfat, with added vitamin D". A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 258 g. That makes it 103% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

Among the 15 items in milk, eggs and dairy, Milk ranks number 11 from the lightest per cup: the next lighter is Egg yolk (243 g) and the next heavier is Curd (245 g).

USDA records a cup of whole milk at a little over the weight of a cup of water because of the dissolved solids. Milk in India varies by animal and dairy; weigh if it matters.

Measuring tip

Liquids such as milk and cream weigh a little more than water, and thick products such as curd, cream cheese and cheese depend on how packed they are. Stir curd before measuring, and weigh cheese when a recipe is sensitive.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of milk?

A US cup of Milk weighs about 244 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 258 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Milk?

500 g is about 2 cups (2.05 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Milk?

100 g is about 6.6 tablespoons or 19.7 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Milk, whole, 3.25% milkfat, with added vitamin D"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
