Vegetables and herbs: cups to grams

10 ingredients, lightest to heaviest.

Vegetables are mostly water and air, so a cup holds little and the weight depends on how finely you cut. Use weight whenever a recipe depends on it, and remember that vegetables shrink a great deal when cooked. This page lists every ingredient in the group, lightest first, with the weight of a US cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon. Open any ingredient for the full table of fractions of a cup, the grams-to-cups table for 50 g to 1 kg and notes on how to measure it.

The lightest in this group is Spinach at 30 g a cup and the heaviest is Onions, chopped at 160 g, so a cup of the heaviest weighs about 5.3 times as much as a cup of the lightest. That ratio is the reason recipes that give only cups can go wrong when the wrong ingredient is swapped in by volume.

An asterisk (*) means the USDA gives only a spoon weight and the cup figure is worked out from it.
