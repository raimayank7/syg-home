Fennel seeds (saunf): cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

The USDA record for "Spices, fennel seed" gives the weight of a tablespoon, not a cup: one level US tablespoon of Fennel seeds weighs about 5.8 g (0.2 oz). A US cup holds 16 tablespoons, so a cup would be about 93 g if you ever needed to measure that much; spices are used by the spoon, which is why the spoon figure is the one to trust.

The USDA measured this one as: whole. If your ingredient is cut or packed differently, expect the weight to move.

Among the 19 items in spices and seasonings, Fennel seeds ranks number 6 from the lightest per cup: the next lighter is Cardamom (93 g) and the next heavier is Cumin seeds (96 g).

Measuring tip

Spices are measured in teaspoons and tablespoons, and the weight per spoon depends on how finely they are ground and how heavily the spoon is heaped. Always level the spoon, and use a fresh spice for the stated flavour.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of fennel seeds?

A US cup of Fennel seeds weighs about 93 g (worked from the spoon weight). A metric 250 ml cup is about 98 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Fennel seeds?

500 g is about 5 and 3/8 cups (5.39 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Fennel seeds?

100 g is about 17.2 tablespoons or 51.7 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Spices, fennel seed"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
