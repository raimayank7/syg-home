Whole moong (green gram), raw: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of whole moong weighs about 207 g (7.3 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Mung beans, mature seeds, raw". A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 219 g. That makes it 87% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

Among the 11 items in dals and beans, Whole moong ranks number 10 from the lightest per cup: the next lighter is Toor dal (205 g) and the next heavier is Navy beans, raw (208 g).

The USDA record is for dried mung beans, whole; split and skinned moong dal weighs differently.

Measuring tip

Dried dals and beans are hard and round, so they pack closely and a cup is heavy. Measure them dry, before soaking; soaked or cooked pulses weigh differently and swell to two or three times their dry volume.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of whole moong?

A US cup of Whole moong weighs about 207 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 219 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Whole moong?

500 g is about 2 and 3/8 cups (2.42 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Whole moong?

100 g is about 7.7 tablespoons or 23.2 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Mung beans, mature seeds, raw"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
