Sugar, granulated: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of sugar, granulated weighs about 200 g (7.1 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Sugars, granulated". A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 211 g. That makes it 84% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

Measuring tip

Sugar and syrups are dense. For syrups, a lightly oiled spoon or cup lets the sweetener slide out, and a cup of syrup is far heavier than a cup of water. Brown sugar is pressed into the cup, white sugar is not.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of sugar, granulated?

A US cup of Sugar, granulated weighs about 200 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 211 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Sugar, granulated?

500 g is about 2 and 1/2 cups (2.5 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Sugar, granulated?

100 g is about 8 tablespoons or 24 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Sugars, granulated"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
