Coconut oil: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of coconut oil weighs about 218 g (7.7 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Oil, coconut". A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 230 g. That makes it 92% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

Measuring tip

Oils and fats are lighter than water by volume, and the same for any cooking oil. Solid fats such as ghee, butter and lard are best weighed, or measured melted for a level cup.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of coconut oil?

A US cup of Coconut oil weighs about 218 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 230 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Coconut oil?

500 g is about 2 and 1/3 cups (2.29 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Coconut oil?

100 g is about 7.3 tablespoons or 22 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Oil, coconut"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
