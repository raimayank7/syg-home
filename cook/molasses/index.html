Molasses: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of molasses weighs about 337 g (11.9 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Molasses". A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 356 g. That makes it 142% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

Measuring tip

Sugar and syrups are dense. For syrups, a lightly oiled spoon or cup lets the sweetener slide out, and a cup of syrup is far heavier than a cup of water. Brown sugar is pressed into the cup, white sugar is not.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of molasses?

A US cup of Molasses weighs about 337 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 356 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Molasses?

500 g is about 1 and 1/2 cups (1.48 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Molasses?

100 g is about 4.7 tablespoons or 14.2 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Molasses"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
