Onion powder: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

The USDA record for "Spices, onion powder" gives the weight of a tablespoon, not a cup: one level US tablespoon of Onion powder weighs about 6.9 g (0.24 oz). A US cup holds 16 tablespoons, so a cup would be about 110 g if you ever needed to measure that much; spices are used by the spoon, which is why the spoon figure is the one to trust.

Measuring tip

Spices are measured in teaspoons and tablespoons, and the weight per spoon depends on how finely they are ground and how heavily the spoon is heaped. Always level the spoon, and use a fresh spice for the stated flavour.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of onion powder?

A US cup of Onion powder weighs about 110 g (worked from the spoon weight). A metric 250 ml cup is about 117 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Onion powder?

500 g is about 4 and 1/2 cups (4.53 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Onion powder?

100 g is about 14.5 tablespoons or 43.5 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Spices, onion powder"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
