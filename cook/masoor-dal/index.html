Masoor dal (red lentils), raw: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of Masoor dal weighs about 192 g (6.8 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Lentils, pink or red, raw". A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 203 g. That makes it 81% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

Measuring tip

Dried dals and beans are hard and round, so they pack closely and a cup is heavy. Measure them dry, before soaking; soaked or cooked pulses weigh differently and swell to two or three times their dry volume.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of Masoor dal?

A US cup of Masoor dal weighs about 192 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 203 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Masoor dal?

500 g is about 2 and 5/8 cups (2.6 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Masoor dal?

100 g is about 8.3 tablespoons or 25 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Lentils, pink or red, raw"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
