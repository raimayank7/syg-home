Almonds (badam), whole: cups, spoons and grams

Weight of a cup, a tablespoon and a teaspoon, and how many cups are in 100 g to 1 kg.

One US cup of almonds weighs about 143 g (5 oz), according to the USDA household-measure record for "Nuts, almonds" (measured as: whole). A metric 250 ml cup holds a little more, about 151 g. That makes it 60% the weight of the same cup of water (about 237 g).

The USDA gives more than one cup weight for this ingredient, because the weight depends on its form. The lightest is a cup of sliced at 92 g, the heaviest a cup of whole at 143 g: a difference of 55%. Read the recipe to see which form it means, and use the matching figure from the table.

Measuring tip

Nuts, seeds and dried fruit depend on how they are cut: whole, chopped and ground nuts weigh very different amounts per cup. Always check which form the recipe means, and pack dried fruit only if the recipe says packed.

Questions

How many grams are in a cup of almonds?

A US cup of Almonds weighs about 143 g. A metric 250 ml cup is about 151 g.

How many cups is 500 g of Almonds?

500 g is about 3 and 1/2 cups (3.5 US cups).

How many tablespoons are in 100 g of Almonds?

100 g is about 11.2 tablespoons or 33.6 teaspoons, taking a US tablespoon as one sixteenth of a US cup.

Where do these figures come from?

They are household measure weights from the USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy database (record "Nuts, almonds"), a public-domain data set. Your own ingredient may differ by a few per cent, so weigh when a recipe needs to be exact.
