Rooftop solar guides

The official rules, in plain words.

These guides explain the central subsidy for home rooftop solar in India, step by step, using only two official documents that were read and dated for this site. They say clearly what was not read, because schemes change. Start with how the subsidy works, then check who can apply and how to apply, and use the calculators to size a system and estimate payback.

  • How the PM-Surya Ghar rooftop solar subsidy worksThe scheme pays part of the cost of a home rooftop solar system. This page explains the official rules.
  • Who can apply for the rooftop solar subsidy?The official rules, in plain words, with the documents the portal says are mandatory.
  • How to apply for the rooftop solar subsidyThe portal shows the journey in nine steps. Here they are, with what each means.
  • How much rooftop solar does a home need?Start from your bill, check against the roof, and compare with where the subsidy stops.
  • Solar payback explainedPayback is a single number that depends on three inputs you control: price, tariff and size.
  • Loans and costs for rooftop solarThe portal publishes a standard loan product for home rooftop solar. Here are its terms as shown on 13 November 2026.
