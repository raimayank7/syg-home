Rooftop solar payback calculator

Enter the size, the quoted price and your electricity rate per unit. The page shows the simple payback with the working.

What it calculates

Simple payback is the amount you pay (the quoted cost minus the central subsidy) divided by what you save each year. Savings are the units the system generates in a year multiplied by the rate you would otherwise pay per unit. The generation figure is the scheme's own: about 100 units per kW per month (Cabinet press release of 29 February 2024: a 3 kW system generates more than 300 units a month on average). The price and the rate are yours: this site prints no prices or tariffs.

A worked example, with made-up prices

Suppose a vendor quotes Rs 2,00,000 for a 3 kW system and your electricity costs Rs 7 a unit. These are example numbers only.

StepWorkingResult
Subsidy2 x 30,000 + 1 x 18,000Rs 78,000
You pay2,00,000 - 78,000Rs 1,22,000
Units a year3 kW x 100 x 123,600 units
Savings a year3,600 x 7Rs 25,200
Payback1,22,000 / 25,200about 4.8 years

What the number leaves out

It is a simple division. It ignores interest if you borrow, changes in electricity rates, slow loss of panel output, maintenance and what the distribution company pays for surplus power under your state's metering rules. It also assumes you use all the generated units at the rate you type. Use it to compare quotes, not as a promise.

These rules were read from the official portal and the scheme guidelines on 13 November 2026. The scheme runs until 31 March 2027, and rules and amounts can change: confirm on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before you pay anyone.

Where do I find my rate per unit?

On your electricity bill. If your bill has slabs, use the rate of the slab your extra units would fall in.

Does the calculator include the subsidy?

Yes, the general central rates, if the box is ticked. State subsidies are not included.
