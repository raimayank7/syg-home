How the PM-Surya Ghar rooftop solar subsidy works

The scheme pays part of the cost of a home rooftop solar system. This page explains the official rules.

The scheme

The Union Cabinet approved PM-Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana on 29 February 2024 with a total outlay of Rs 75,021 crore; the Prime Minister launched it on 13 February 2024. Its aims include rooftop solar for one crore (ten million) households and free or low-cost electricity up to 300 units a month. According to the guidelines the scheme is implemented until 31 March 2027, and the National Portal's home page shows a countdown to that closing date.

The maximum for a residential consumer is Rs 78,000 (FAQ on the National Portal). The guidelines derive these amounts from a benchmark cost of Rs 50,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and Rs 45,000 per kW for the additional kW (Rs 55,000 and Rs 49,500 in special category states): 60% of the benchmark for the first 2 kWp and 40% for the additional one. The benchmark may be revised at the scheme's mid-term review. Use the subsidy calculator for any size.

Conditions that matter

  • Residential only. No CFA is given to non-residential consumers such as commercial, industrial or government connections.
  • Grid-connected on a roof, terrace, balcony or elevated structure, tagged to a residential connection of the local DISCOM. Off-grid installations are not eligible.
  • Domestic Content Requirement. The modules must be domestically manufactured from domestically made cells, or the installation is ineligible. The "Give It Up" option (not taking the subsidy) is free of this condition.
  • One time. A system gets CFA only once; a relocated system is not eligible again. An earlier MNRE-subsidised plant that is later enlarged can get additional CFA only for the balance up to 3 kW in total.
  • State subsidy may be added by a state, integrated with the portal. Amounts are not on the national pages.

Sources, all read on 13 November 2026: the National Portal pmsuryaghar.gov.in (home page, How to Apply, Financing Options, FAQs); the scheme guidelines "Central Financial Assistance to Residential Consumers" downloaded from the portal's Scheme Guidelines page; the PIB press release of 29 February 2024; the MNRE criteria annexure (PIB, August 2024). These rules were read from the official portal and the scheme guidelines on 13 November 2026. The scheme runs until 31 March 2027, and rules and amounts can change: confirm on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before you pay anyone.

Who pays the subsidy and when?

The central government. The guidelines describe an e-token: after the DISCOM's physical inspection and approval on the portal, the e-token is activated with the CFA for the installed capacity, and the consumer redeems it on the portal.

What happens after 31 March 2027?

This site has read nothing about it. Check the portal for any extension.
