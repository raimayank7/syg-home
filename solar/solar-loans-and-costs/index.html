Loans and costs for rooftop solar

The portal publishes a standard loan product for home rooftop solar. Here are its terms as shown on 13 November 2026.

The standard loan product

The portal's Financing Options page describes a standard loan product formulated by the Indian Banks' Association and offered by public sector banks (the page lists the banks and their nodal officers). It has two columns:

Maximum amountRs 2 lakhRs 6 lakh
Ageup to 65 years (repaid before 70)same conditions
PANnot requiredmandatory
Margin (your share)minimum 10% of project costminimum 20% of project cost
Interestfloating, RBI repo rate + 0.50%"Rate of interest 6.00% p.a. (as on date)" for home loan customers (same as home loan); for others the home loan rate + 100 bps
Tenureup to 120 months including moratorium; no minimum; no prepayment chargesame
Processing feezerozero
Moratorium6 months from disbursementsame
Net annual incomenot requiredminimum Rs 3 lakh

Other points on the page: savings account with the bank is mandatory; the loan is disbursed to the vendor in two tranches; no mandatory insurance for loans up to 3 kW; applicants aged 65 or more can add a co-borrower; the application starts from your login on the portal. The page quotes the subsidy as Rs 30,000 (1 kW), Rs 60,000 (2 kW) and Rs 78,000 (3 kW) and says the claim is made on the portal. The FAQ adds that the loan facility is available only for the residential category.

A portal announcement says that for loan cases, the subsidy redeemed on or after 14 August 2026 is credited to the loan account; if loan details do not arrive from Jan Samarth within 15 days of inspection approval, the consumer can give savings bank account details instead.

These are the portal's published terms. Your bank decides on your application, and rates move with the repo rate.

Costs

The guidelines fix a benchmark cost (Rs 50,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and Rs 45,000 for the additional kW; Rs 55,000 and Rs 49,500 in special states) from which the subsidy is computed, and say the Ministry will publish indicative costs of components so consumers are not overcharged. Actual prices are agreed with the vendor, and this site prints none. Ask for the total price with the subsidy shown separately, what is included, the warranty, and the payment schedule.

Sources, all read on 13 November 2026: the National Portal pmsuryaghar.gov.in (home page, How to Apply, Financing Options, FAQs); the scheme guidelines "Central Financial Assistance to Residential Consumers" downloaded from the portal's Scheme Guidelines page; the PIB press release of 29 February 2024; the MNRE criteria annexure (PIB, August 2024). These rules were read from the official portal and the scheme guidelines on 13 November 2026. The scheme runs until 31 March 2027, and rules and amounts can change: confirm on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before you pay anyone.

Is the loan part of the scheme?

It is offered through the National Portal, integrated with the Jan Samarth portal.

Does the subsidy reduce my loan?

For loan cases redeemed on or after 14 August 2026 the portal says the subsidy is credited to the loan account.
