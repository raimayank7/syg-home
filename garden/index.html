Questions people ask

Which region should I choose?

Choose by your winter. If you get cold winters with fog or frost, choose the northern and central plains. If winter is mild and you never see frost, choose south and coastal. If you live above about 1,000 metres, choose hills.

Are the sowing months exact?

No. They are the usual windows for a broad region. Your town may be two or three weeks earlier or later. A local nursery will know.

Can I grow vegetables without a garden?

Yes. Every vegetable on this site has pot sizes on its page, and the balcony and terrace guide explains how to start.

What is the easiest vegetable to start with?

Methi, radish and palak in the cool months, and okra or amaranth in the warm months. They are quick and forgiving.
