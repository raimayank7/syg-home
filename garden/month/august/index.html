What to sow in August in India

Vegetables, herbs and flowers to sow or plant in August, for the northern plains, the south and the hills, with the garden jobs of the month.

North and central plains

Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, West Bengal plains and places with a similar cold winter and hot summer.

TomatoCapsicumCauliflowerCabbageBroccoliCarrotRadish (mooli)French beansMint (pudina)

Jobs this month:

  • Sow mid-season cauliflower, cabbage and broccoli in trays, and the first carrots and radish late in the month.
  • Sow French beans for an autumn crop.
  • Feed the monsoon crops with compost, since rain washes nutrients out of pots.
  • Pinch chrysanthemums and marigolds to make them bushy.
  • Start clearing space and preparing soil for the winter garden, the main vegetable season of the plains.

South and coastal India

Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Goa, coastal Maharashtra and Odisha: places where winter is mild and frost is unknown.

CauliflowerBroccoliCarrotRadish (mooli)BeetrootGarlicMint (pudina)

Sow carrots, beetroot, radish and beans. Plant the second round of gourds where the season allows.

Hills

Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Kashmir, Sikkim, Darjeeling, the Nilgiris and other places above about 1,000 metres, with cold winters and mild summers.

BroccoliRadish (mooli)Turnip (shalgam)Spinach (palak)Fenugreek (methi)Coriander (dhania)LettuceMustard greens (sarson)

Sow mustard greens, turnip, spinach and lettuce for autumn and early winter.
