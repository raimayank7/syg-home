How to grow radish (mooli)

मूली. When to sow it in each part of India, how deep, how far apart, what size of pot, and what to watch for.

Green months are the usual sowing or planting time. Dates shift by a few weeks from place to place.

How to sow and grow

Method
Sow the seed straight where the plant will grow.
Depth
1 cm
Spacing
Thin to 5 to 8 cm apart
In a pot
A box or pot 25 to 30 cm deep
Sprouts in
3 to 7 days
Harvest
25 to 45 days for quick varieties, up to 60 for long white ones
Sun
Full sun: at least 6 hours a day

Tips from the garden

  • Radish is the fastest root crop and a good first vegetable for children.
  • Sow a short row every two weeks through the season, and you will have a steady supply and no glut.
  • Harvest on time. Old radishes turn hollow and sharp in taste.

What can go wrong

All leaves and no root usually means too much shade, too much nitrogen or plants left crowded.

Questions people ask

When is radish sown in north India?

In the plains of north and central India, the usual sowing or planting months for radish are August to January.

How long does radish take to grow?

Usually 25 to 45 days for quick varieties, up to 60 for long white ones.

Can radish be grown in a pot?

Yes. A box or pot 25 to 30 cm deep.
