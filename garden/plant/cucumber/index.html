How to sow and grow

Method
Sow the seed straight where the plant will grow.
Depth
1.5 cm
Spacing
One or two plants per container
In a pot
A tub or grow bag of 25 litres or more, with a net to climb
Sprouts in
4 to 8 days
Harvest
45 to 60 days
Sun
Full sun: at least 6 hours a day

Tips from the garden

  • Keep the soil evenly moist. Dry spells make the fruits bitter.
  • Let the vine climb. Fruits grown off the ground are straighter and cleaner.
  • Pick often. One fruit left to turn yellow and seedy stops the vine from making more.

What can go wrong

Yellow angular spots on the leaves that turn brown are downy mildew, common in humid weather. Remove affected leaves and avoid wetting the foliage.

Questions people ask

When is cucumber sown in north India?

In the plains of north and central India, the usual sowing or planting months for cucumber are February and March, June and July.

How long does cucumber take to grow?

Usually 45 to 60 days.

Can cucumber be grown in a pot?

Yes. A tub or grow bag of 25 litres or more, with a net to climb.
