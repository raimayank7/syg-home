Winter Activities: calories burned

52 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 52 activities under "Winter Activities". Their MET values run from 2.0 (Ice fishing) to 16.0 (Skiing, cross country, 12-16 mph, elite skier, racing). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 4 are sedentary or light, 6 moderate and 42 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate. Use the calculator for your own weight and time.
