Walking: calories burned

93 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 93 activities under "Walking". Their MET values run from 1.5 (Walking, with a walker or step-to gait on treadmill, 0.7 mph (1.1 km/h) to 16.3 (Climbing hills, no load, steep grade (10-40%), 1.8 to 5.0 mph). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 12 are sedentary or light, 42 moderate and 39 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate.
