Basketball, general: calories burned

7.5 METs, vigorous intensity. Kilocalories by body weight and time.

The 2024 Adult Compendium gives Basketball, general (code 15055, group "Sports") a standard value of 7.5 METs, which the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans class as vigorous intensity. That is 2.0 times the 3.8 METs the Compendium gives for walking at 2.8 to 3.4 mph on level ground, and it ranks number 97 of the 156 activities in the group when they are listed from the lowest MET value.

In 30 minutes the activity adds up to 225 MET-minutes (MET x minutes). The weekly guidelines for adults, about 500 to 1,000 MET-minutes, would be reached by 2.2 sessions of this length at the lower figure. To use about 100 kilocalories takes roughly 13 minutes at 60 kg, 11 at 70 kg and 10 at 80 kg, by the Compendium conversion METs x 3.5 x kg / 200 per minute.
