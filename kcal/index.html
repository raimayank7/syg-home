Calories burned calculator

Pick an activity, enter your weight and time: kilocalories from standard MET values.

What this calculator does

Pick an activity from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities (1,110 rows), enter your body weight and the time, and it gives the kilocalories as MET x 3.5 x body weight in kg / 200 x minutes, the Compendium's own conversion. It also shows the MET-minutes and the intensity class, and can work backwards to the minutes needed for a target number of kilocalories. The MET values are the Compendium's standard values, unchanged.

How to read the answer

The number is an estimate for an average adult of your weight, not a measurement of you: the Compendium's authors say it was not developed to determine the precise energy cost of an activity within an individual. Use it to compare activities and to see how weight and time change the result. See accuracy and limits.
