Bicycling: calories burned

44 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 44 activities under "Bicycling". Their MET values run from 2.3 (Bicycling, eccentric only, 100 to 149 W) to 16.8 (Bicycling, >20 mph, racing, not drafting). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 1 are sedentary or light, 13 moderate and 30 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate.
