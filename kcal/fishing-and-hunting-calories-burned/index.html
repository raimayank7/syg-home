Fishing and hunting: calories burned

37 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 37 activities under "Fishing and hunting". Their MET values run from 1.0 (Sitting in boat, passenger) to 9.8 (Hunting large game, dragging carcass). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 15 are sedentary or light, 17 moderate and 5 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate. Use the calculator for your own weight and time.
