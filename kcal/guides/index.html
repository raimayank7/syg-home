Calories burned guides

The formula, the units and the limits.

These guides explain the formula behind the calorie estimates, what a MET is, what the physical activity guidelines say about weekly minutes, and how far the figures can be trusted. They use only the Compendium of Physical Activities and the US Physical Activity Guidelines, both read and dated for this site.

An estimate. MET values are averages for adults, not measurements of you. Treat every figure as a rough estimate. This site gives general information, not medical advice, and is independent of the Compendium authors and of the US government. See accuracy and limits.
