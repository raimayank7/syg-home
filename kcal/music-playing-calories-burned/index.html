Music Playing: calories burned

22 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 22 activities under "Music Playing". Their MET values run from 1.8 (Accordion, sitting) to 8.3 (Drumming, concert/live show). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 14 are sedentary or light, 7 moderate and 1 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate.
