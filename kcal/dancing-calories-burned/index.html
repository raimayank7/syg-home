Dancing: calories burned

28 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 28 activities under "Dancing". Their MET values run from 3.0 (Ballroom, slow, examples: waltz, foxtrot, slow dancing, samba tango, r) to 13.0 (Ballroom dance, DanceSport competition (modern waltz, tango, Viennese ). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 0 are sedentary or light, 15 moderate and 13 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate.
