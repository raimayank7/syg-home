Water Activities: calories burned

86 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 86 activities under "Water Activities". Their MET values run from 1.3 (Boating, power, passenger, light) to 15.8 (Skindiving, fast). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 8 are sedentary or light, 32 moderate and 46 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate. Use the calculator for your own weight and time.

Putting the numbers to use

The average MET value of the activities in this group is 6.9. For a person of 70 kg, the busiest activity listed, Skindiving, fast (15.8 METs), uses about 1,161 kcal in an hour, and the gentlest, Boating, power, passenger, light (1.3 METs), about 96 kcal in an hour. To use up 100 kcal at 70 kg would take about 5 minutes of the first and about 63 minutes of the second. The gap between them is the reason the same hour can look very different on a fitness tracker depending on what you do.

These are standard values for a typical adult, measured as an average over many people. A heavier person burns more at the same MET value and a lighter person burns less, which is why the table has three body weights. Your own result also depends on fitness, technique, terrain and how hard you work, so treat the figures as a guide for comparing activities and not as an exact count. They do not include the calories you would have used anyway at rest in that time, a point explained in the guide on how the figures are calculated.
