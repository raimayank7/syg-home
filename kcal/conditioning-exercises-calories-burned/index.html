Conditioning exercises: calories burned

86 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 86 activities under "Conditioning exercises". Their MET values run from 1.3 (Whirlpool, sitting) to 18.0 (Ski ergometer, cross country, double poling, fast to maximum speed). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 16 are sedentary or light, 30 moderate and 40 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate.
