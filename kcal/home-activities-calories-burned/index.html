Home Activities: calories burned

78 activities with standard MET values and kilocalories for 30 minutes.

The 2024 Adult Compendium lists 78 activities under "Home Activities". Their MET values run from 1.3 (Knitting, sewing, light effort, wrapping presents, sitting) to 9.0 (Moving household items upstairs, carrying boxes or furniture). By the intensity classes of the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 38 are sedentary or light, 37 moderate and 3 vigorous. The table shows the kilocalories used in 30 minutes at three body weights (55, 70 and 85 kg), worked out as MET x 3.5 x kg / 200 x 30. Every figure is an estimate.
