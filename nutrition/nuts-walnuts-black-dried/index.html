Nuts, walnuts, black, dried: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Nuts, walnuts, black, dried provide about 619 kcal (2,590 kJ), 24.1 g of protein, 59.3 g of fat and 9.6 g of carbohydrate, including 6.8 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Nuts, walnuts, black, dried". Water makes up 4.6 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 6% from carbohydrate, 80% from fat and 14% from protein.
