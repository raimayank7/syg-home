Milk, eggs and dairy: nutrition facts

33 foods, per 100 g.

This page lists the 33 foods of the group "Milk, eggs and dairy" with their energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate and fibre per 100 grams, all from the USDA SR Legacy database. The median food here has 237 kcal per 100 g, with a range from 40 to 669 kcal, so a gram of one food can carry many times the energy of a gram of another. Open any food for its full table of minerals and vitamins and its household portions.

Comparing per 100 g is fair but not always practical: a serving of a leafy vegetable weighs far less than a serving of nuts. Use the portion tables on the food pages to compare what you actually handle in the kitchen. This is general information and not dietary advice.
