Oils and fats: nutrition facts

8 foods, per 100 g.

This page lists the 8 foods of the group "Oils and fats" with their energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate and fibre per 100 grams, all from the USDA SR Legacy database. The median food here has 884 kcal per 100 g, with a range from 717 to 902 kcal, so a gram of one food can carry many times the energy of a gram of another. Open any food for its full table of minerals and vitamins and its household portions.

Comparing per 100 g is fair but not always practical: a serving of a leafy vegetable weighs far less than a serving of nuts. Use the portion tables on the food pages to compare what you actually handle in the kitchen. This is general information and not dietary advice.

Highest in this group (per 100 g)

  • Energy: Lard (902 kcal), Oil, coconut (892 kcal), Oil, olive, salad or cooking (884 kcal), Groundnut (peanut) oil (884 kcal), Oil, sesame, salad or cooking (884 kcal)
  • Protein: Butter, without salt (0.8 g), Ghee (USDA lists it as butter oil, anhydrous) (0.3 g), Lard (0 g), Oil, coconut (0 g), Oil, olive, salad or cooking (0 g)
  • Fibre: Ghee (USDA lists it as butter oil, anhydrous) (0 g), Butter, without salt (0 g), Lard (0 g), Oil, coconut (0 g), Oil, olive, salad or cooking (0 g)

From 100 grams to a real portion

The figures in the table are for 100 grams, which is a handy way to compare foods but not a normal portion. A 30 g portion of Lard, the highest in the group at 902 kcal per 100 g, supplies about 271 kcal, while 30 g of Butter, without salt, the lowest at 717 kcal per 100 g, supplies about 215 kcal. A 200 g portion would be 1,804 kcal and 1,434 kcal respectively. Scale the figures for the amount you eat, not the amount in the table.

For Lard, the energy comes roughly 0% from carbohydrate, 100% from fat and 0% from protein, using the general factors of 4, 9 and 4 kilocalories per gram. This is an approximation, since alcohol, fibre and other components also count, but it shows what kind of food it is: a high-carbohydrate, high-fat or high-protein one. The split changes widely within a group, so open each food page for the detail, including minerals and vitamins and household portions such as a cup or a slice.
