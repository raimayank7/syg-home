Fruits: nutrition facts

95 foods, per 100 g.

This page lists the 95 foods of the group "Fruits" with their energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate and fibre per 100 grams, all from the USDA SR Legacy database. The median food here has 57 kcal per 100 g, with a range from 21 to 349 kcal, so a gram of one food can carry many times the energy of a gram of another. Open any food for its full table of minerals and vitamins and its household portions.

Comparing per 100 g is fair but not always practical: a serving of a leafy vegetable weighs far less than a serving of nuts. Use the portion tables on the food pages to compare what you actually handle in the kitchen. This is general information and not dietary advice.

Highest in this group (per 100 g)

  • Energy: Goji berries, dried (349 kcal), Candied fruit (322 kcal), Currants, zante, dried (290 kcal), Longans, dried (286 kcal), Jujube, Chinese, fresh, dried (281 kcal)
  • Protein: Goji berries, dried (14.3 g), Longans, dried (4.9 g), Jujube, Chinese, fresh, dried (4.7 g), Litchis, dried (3.8 g), Baobab powder (3.7 g)
  • Fibre: Baobab powder (44.5 g), Persimmons, japanese, dried (14.5 g), Goji berries, dried (13 g), Passion-fruit, (granadilla), purple, raw (10.4 g), Elderberries, raw (7 g)

From 100 grams to a real portion

The figures in the table are for 100 grams, which is a handy way to compare foods but not a normal portion. A 30 g portion of Goji berries, dried, the highest in the group at 349 kcal per 100 g, supplies about 105 kcal, while 30 g of Rhubarb, raw, the lowest at 21 kcal per 100 g, supplies about 6 kcal. A 200 g portion would be 698 kcal and 42 kcal respectively. Scale the figures for the amount you eat, not the amount in the table.

For Goji berries, dried, the energy comes roughly 84% from carbohydrate, 1% from fat and 15% from protein, using the general factors of 4, 9 and 4 kilocalories per gram. This is an approximation, since alcohol, fibre and other components also count, but it shows what kind of food it is: a high-carbohydrate, high-fat or high-protein one. The split changes widely within a group, so open each food page for the detail, including minerals and vitamins and household portions such as a cup or a slice.
