Spices and seasonings: nutrition facts

35 foods, per 100 g.

This page lists the 35 foods of the group "Spices and seasonings" with their energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate and fibre per 100 grams, all from the USDA SR Legacy database. The median food here has 288 kcal per 100 g, with a range from 0 to 525 kcal, so a gram of one food can carry many times the energy of a gram of another. Open any food for its full table of minerals and vitamins and its household portions.

Comparing per 100 g is fair but not always practical: a serving of a leafy vegetable weighs far less than a serving of nuts. Use the portion tables on the food pages to compare what you actually handle in the kitchen. This is general information and not dietary advice.
