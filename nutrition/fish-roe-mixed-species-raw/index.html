Fish, roe, mixed species, raw: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Fish, roe, mixed species, raw provide about 143 kcal (598 kJ), 22.3 g of protein, 6.4 g of fat and 1.5 g of carbohydrate, including 0 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Fish, roe, mixed species, raw". Water makes up 67.7 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 4% from carbohydrate, 38% from fat and 58% from protein.
