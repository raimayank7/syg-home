Nuts, hickorynuts, dried: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Nuts, hickorynuts, dried provide about 657 kcal (2,749 kJ), 12.7 g of protein, 64.4 g of fat and 18.2 g of carbohydrate, including 6.4 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Nuts, hickorynuts, dried". Water makes up 2.6 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 10% from carbohydrate, 82% from fat and 7% from protein.
