Nutrition number guides

How to read the numbers on this site.

Nutrition numbers are easy to misread: per 100 g or per serving, raw or cooked, kilocalories or kilojoules. These guides explain each of those and where the figures on this site come from. They give information only, not dietary advice.

  • Nutrition per 100 g or per serving?The same food looks very different per 100 g and per serving. Both are correct; they answer different questions.
  • How the calories in food are countedFood energy is not measured by burning every food. It is mostly calculated from protein, fat and carbohydrate.
  • Raw and cooked foods: why the numbers changeCooking does not remove calories from lentils. It adds water, so each 100 g weighs less in energy.
  • Why nutrition values differ between sourcesTwo reliable sources can give different numbers for one food. This guide explains the common reasons.
  • About the data: USDA FoodData CentralEvery number on this site comes from one published source. This page names it and says what it covers.
