Milk, dry, nonfat, regular, without added vitamin A and vitamin D: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

Also known in India as Milk powder (nonfat dry milk). 100 grams of Milk powder (nonfat dry milk) provide about 362 kcal (1,515 kJ), 36.2 g of protein, 0.8 g of fat and 52 g of carbohydrate, including 0 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Milk, dry, nonfat, regular, without added vitamin A and vitamin D". Water makes up 3.2 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 58% from carbohydrate, 2% from fat and 40% from protein.
