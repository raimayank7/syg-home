Fish, salmon, sockeye, raw: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Fish, salmon, sockeye, raw provide about 131 kcal (548 kJ), 22.2 g of protein, 4.7 g of fat and 0 g of carbohydrate, including 0 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Fish, salmon, sockeye, raw". Water makes up 72.5 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 0% from carbohydrate, 32% from fat and 68% from protein.
