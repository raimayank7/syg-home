Soy flour, full-fat, raw: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Soy flour, full-fat, raw provide about 434 kcal (1,816 kJ), 37.8 g of protein, 20.6 g of fat and 31.9 g of carbohydrate, including 9.6 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Soy flour, full-fat, raw". Water makes up 5.2 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 27% from carbohydrate, 40% from fat and 33% from protein.
