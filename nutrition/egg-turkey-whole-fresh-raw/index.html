Egg, turkey, whole, fresh, raw: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Egg, turkey, whole, fresh, raw provide about 171 kcal (715 kJ), 13.7 g of protein, 11.9 g of fat and 1.1 g of carbohydrate, including 0 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Egg, turkey, whole, fresh, raw". Water makes up 72.5 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 3% from carbohydrate, 64% from fat and 33% from protein.
