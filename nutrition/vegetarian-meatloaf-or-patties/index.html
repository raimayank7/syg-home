Vegetarian meatloaf or patties: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Vegetarian meatloaf or patties provide about 197 kcal (824 kJ), 21 g of protein, 9 g of fat and 8 g of carbohydrate, including 4.6 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Vegetarian meatloaf or patties". Water makes up 58 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 16% from carbohydrate, 41% from fat and 43% from protein.
