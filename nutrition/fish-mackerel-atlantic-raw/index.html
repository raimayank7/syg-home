Fish, mackerel, Atlantic, raw: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Fish, mackerel, Atlantic, raw provide about 205 kcal (858 kJ), 18.6 g of protein, 13.9 g of fat and 0 g of carbohydrate, including 0 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Fish, mackerel, Atlantic, raw". Water makes up 63.5 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 0% from carbohydrate, 63% from fat and 37% from protein.
