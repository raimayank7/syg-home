Spices, cinnamon, ground: calories and nutrition

Per 100 g and per portion, from USDA data.

100 grams of Spices, cinnamon, ground provide about 247 kcal (1,033 kJ), 4 g of protein, 1.2 g of fat and 80.6 g of carbohydrate, including 53.1 g of dietary fibre, according to the USDA FoodData Central record "Spices, cinnamon, ground". Water makes up 10.6 g of every 100 g. Using the general 4-4-9 energy factors for protein, carbohydrate and fat, the energy works out at approximately 92% from carbohydrate, 3% from fat and 5% from protein.
