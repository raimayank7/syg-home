About these guides

An electricity bill is easy to pay and hard to read. These guides explain it part by part: what a unit is, how slab rates turn units into rupees, what the fixed charge is for, which appliances use the most, and what to check when a bill looks wrong.

They are written for households, in plain words, and they apply across India, because every state's bill is built from the same parts even though the figures differ. For the figures themselves, each guide sends you to your own bill or your supplier's tariff schedule; the only rates printed on this site are those we have read in an official order, at present for Punjab.

Where to start

  • To understand your bill: how to read your bill, slab rates and fixed charge and load.
  • A bill that looks too high: the checklist, then the meter reading calculator.
  • To use less: how to reduce the bill, then the pages on air conditioners, geysers and star labels.
  • Thinking ahead: smart and prepaid meters and rooftop solar.
Do the guides apply to my state?

Yes for how bills work. Rates, duties, subsidies and rules differ by state and supplier, so take those from your own bill and your supplier's website.

Can I rely on the wattage figures?

They are typical values for planning. Check the rating plate or star label of your own appliance.
