Punjab (PSPCL) domestic electricity tariff, FY 2026-27

The domestic supply rates of Punjab State Power Corporation Limited for FY 2026-27, as published in the official tariff table, with worked examples.

Source and date. PSPCL, "Existing Tariff and the new tariff for FY 2026-27" (Annexure-A), published by PSPCL (official PDF), which reproduces the tariff fixed in the PSERC Tariff Order for FY 2026-27 for PSPCL, issued on 6 March 2026 (Petition No. 70 of 2025). Read by us on 12 November 2026. Valid 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Tariffs can be revised: the regulator's order and your own bill prevail. This is an independent site, not connected with PSPCL or PSERC.

Compared with the year before, the official table shows the rate for the first 300 units for the smallest connections moving from ₹5.40 to ₹3.85 per unit, a fall of ₹1.55, and the rate above 300 units from ₹7.75 to ₹7.05. The fixed charge per kW is unchanged for loads up to 2 kW and lower for the two larger bands.

Two rules stated in the official table

  1. Fixed charge on 80% of the load. The table's footnote says the fixed charge "shall be levied on 80% of the sanctioned/contracted load", or on the actual load if that is found to be higher. So a 5 kW connection is charged on 4 kW.
  2. Other charges apply in addition. The footnote says that fuel and power purchase adjustment surcharge (FPPAS), voltage surcharge or rebate, and time-of-day tariff apply in addition to the energy charges, under the general conditions of the tariff order.

What this table does not include

The official table gives energy and fixed charges only. A PSPCL bill also carries items that are not in it, and which we therefore do not state:

  • the fuel adjustment (FPPAS) amount for the month;
  • electricity duty, cesses and any municipal levy charged by the state;
  • meter and service rentals;
  • any subsidy or free-units scheme of the Government of Punjab, and its conditions;
  • the billing cycle of your connection, and arrears or adjustments.

Your own bill shows each of these. Use the extra boxes in the bill calculator to add them.

For a bill covering two months, the calculator doubles the 300-unit limit and the fixed charge, which is the usual way bimonthly bills are worked; check your own bill's working.

Which load band am I in?

Your sanctioned load is printed on the bill, in kW. It decides both the fixed charge per kW and the rate for the first 300 units. If the load on your bill is well above what your home uses, a lower load may move you to a lower band; if it is too low, the supplier may revise it upward from your recorded demand. See fixed charge and sanctioned load.

Use the official rates in the calculator

Open the bill calculator, choose "Punjab (PSPCL), domestic supply" under Tariff, pick your load band, and type your units. The slab rates and fixed charge are filled in from the table above.

Checking the source yourself

The table is Annexure-A of a PSPCL commercial circular on the tariff for FY 2026-27, available on PSPCL's document site, and the full Tariff Order is published by the Punjab State Electricity Regulatory Commission. PSPCL's own website and bill are the final word on what you are charged.

What is the PSPCL domestic rate per unit in FY 2026-27?

For loads up to 2 kW, ₹3.85 per unit for the first 300 units and ₹7.05 above 300, under the official table valid 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Larger loads pay a higher rate for the first 300 units. Fuel adjustment, duty and other items are added on the bill.

What is the fixed charge for a domestic connection in Punjab?

₹50 per kW per month up to 2 kW, ₹70 per kW above 2 and up to 7 kW, and ₹100 per kW above 7 and up to 20 kW, levied on 80% of the sanctioned load, under the FY 2026-27 table.

Does this page include the free units scheme?

No. The official tariff table does not contain it, so it is not stated here. See your bill or the announcements of the Government of Punjab and PSPCL.
