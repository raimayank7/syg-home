Dairy Products & Eggs: how long food keeps

46 foods from the FoodKeeper data.

Of the 46 foods, 44 have a refrigerator time in the data, 34 have a freezer time and 4 have a pantry time; the rest have only tips, or times counted another way. A food that has no time for a place is one where FoodKeeper gives none, which is different from saying that place is safe.

This category of the USDA FSIS FoodKeeper data contains 46 foods. For each one the table shows the first storage time the data list (refrigerator first, then pantry, then freezer), and the food's page gives every time, the counting point (purchase, opening or thawing) and the tips FSIS gives. Times are ranges and general guidance, not a promise about a particular package: read how to read the times first. Foods that are not in the FoodKeeper data are not listed, and this site has no number for them.
