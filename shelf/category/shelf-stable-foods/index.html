Shelf Stable Foods: how long food keeps

104 foods from the FoodKeeper data.

Of the 104 foods, 46 have a refrigerator time in the data, 21 have a freezer time and 99 have a pantry time; the rest have only tips, or times counted another way. A food that has no time for a place is one where FoodKeeper gives none, which is different from saying that place is safe.

This category of the USDA FSIS FoodKeeper data contains 104 foods. For each one the table shows the first storage time the data list (refrigerator first, then pantry, then freezer), and the food's page gives every time, the counting point (purchase, opening or thawing) and the tips FSIS gives. Times are ranges and general guidance, not a promise about a particular package: read how to read the times first. Foods that are not in the FoodKeeper data are not listed, and this site has no number for them.
