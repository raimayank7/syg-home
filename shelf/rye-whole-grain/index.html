How long does Rye, whole grain last?

Storage times from the USDA FoodKeeper data.

This page gives the storage times that the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) lists in its FoodKeeper data for Rye, whole grain, in the category "Grains, Beans & Pasta". In the data: pantry: 6 months; freezer: 12 months.

Storage times in the FoodKeeper data

Where and whenHow long it keeps
Pantry6 months
Freezer12 months

The times are ranges from FSIS: the first number is the shorter time and the second the longer one, and the heading says whether the clock starts at the date of purchase, at opening or at thawing, as FoodKeeper labels each column. They are about storage, not about how food looks or smells: read how to read the FoodKeeper times before relying on a number. This site gives information only; the package date, your local food safety authority and your own judgement decide.

Questions

How long does Rye, whole grain last in the refrigerator?

The FoodKeeper data give no time for the refrigerator for Rye, whole grain.

How long does Rye, whole grain last in the freezer?

FoodKeeper lists freezer for Rye, whole grain: 12 months.

How long does Rye, whole grain last in the pantry?

FoodKeeper lists pantry for Rye, whole grain: 6 months.

Where do these times come from?

From the USDA FSIS FoodKeeper data, version 128 (modified 6 September 2018). The data are general guidance for storage; the package label and local food safety rules take priority.

General information, not food safety advice for your case. Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, FoodKeeper data (English JSON, data version 128, modified 6 September 2018), https://www.fsis.usda.gov/shared/data/EN/foodkeeper.json, listed on data.gov ("FSIS - FoodKeeper Data", dataset last updated 22 January 2025), read on 13 November 2026.
