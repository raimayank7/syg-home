How long does food last?

Search 661 foods in the USDA FoodKeeper data.

    Type a food to see how long the USDA says it keeps in the pantry, refrigerator and freezer. The numbers come from the FSIS FoodKeeper data: 661 foods in 25 categories, each with its own page showing every storage time (counted from purchase, opening or thawing, as the data label them) and the storage tips FSIS gives.

    These are general guidance times from American data (version 128, 2018), not a statement that a particular food is safe. Read how to read the times and the four food safety guidelines first; the package label and your local food safety rules come before any number here.
