Food storage guides

How to read the times, and what they leave out.

A storage time is easy to misread: it can start at purchase, at opening or at thawing, it is a range, and it is guidance rather than a guarantee. These guides explain the columns of the FoodKeeper data, the four food safety guidelines published by FSIS, and the data itself.

  • How to read the FoodKeeper storage timesA storage time is a range with a starting point. Here is what each part means.
  • The four food safety guidelines from FSISStorage times are only one part of food safety. FSIS lists four guidelines that apply at every step.
