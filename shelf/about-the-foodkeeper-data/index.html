About the data: FoodKeeper

Every time and temperature on this site comes from one published data file. This page describes it.

The source

FoodKeeper is the data behind the FSIS FoodKeeper application, which, according to its data.gov listing, educates users about food and beverage storage to help them maximise freshness and quality. The English data file is published at https://www.fsis.usda.gov/shared/data/EN/foodkeeper.json (Spanish and Portuguese versions exist) and is listed on data.gov as "FSIS - FoodKeeper Data", dataset last updated 22 January 2025. The file we used is data version 128, modified 6 September 2018 (the file's own version sheet). It has 661 products in 25 categories and a table of cooking rows and tips. We read the file on 13 November 2026; the FSIS site refuses automated downloads, so it was saved through a web browser.

Copyright

FoodKeeper is published by a US federal agency. Works of the US federal government are not subject to copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105). We credit FSIS as the source on every page.

What we added

Page layout, the plain-language explanation of columns, comparisons inside a category (for example a median of the longest refrigerator times, which uses months of 30 days and weeks of 7 days for the comparison only), and conversion of Fahrenheit to Celsius by formula. We did not change any stored time. Products that have neither a storage time nor a tip in the data are not on this site.

Limits

The data are general guidance, American, and from 2018 (the listing was refreshed in 2025, but the file's version sheet ends at version 128). They do not describe your kitchen, and the package label comes first. This site is independent and is not connected with the USDA.

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, FoodKeeper data (English JSON, data version 128, modified 6 September 2018), https://www.fsis.usda.gov/shared/data/EN/foodkeeper.json, listed on data.gov ("FSIS - FoodKeeper Data", dataset last updated 22 January 2025), read on 13 November 2026.

Why version 128 if the listing was updated in 2025?

The file's version sheet lists 128 as its current version, dated 6 September 2018. The 2025 date is the data.gov listing's update date.
