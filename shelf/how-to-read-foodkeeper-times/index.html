How to read the FoodKeeper storage times

A storage time is a range with a starting point. Here is what each part means.

The columns

The FoodKeeper data give, for each product, up to nine storage times. Their names in the data are:

ColumnMeaning (as named in the data)
Pantrytime in the pantry
Pantry, counted from the date of purchasepantry time with the clock starting at the date of purchase
Pantry, after openingpantry time once the package is opened
Refrigeratortime in the refrigerator
Refrigerator, counted from the date of purchaserefrigerator time from the date of purchase
Refrigerator, after openingrefrigerator time once opened
Refrigerator, after thawingrefrigerator time after thawing
Freezertime in the freezer
Freezer, counted from the date of purchasefreezer time from the date of purchase

The data file also has tips for some of these (for example "may be left at room temperature for 1 - 2 days" for butter in the pantry). Where a tip exists, it is shown on the food's page word for word.

Ranges

Most times are a range of two numbers and a unit (days, weeks, months or years): "1 to 2 months". The metric can also be "indefinitely", "not recommended", "when ripe" or "package use-by date", which the pages show as written. A "not recommended" for a place means FoodKeeper does not recommend storing the food there.

What a time does not tell you

It does not say that a food is safe or unsafe on a given day. It is general guidance for storing the product, and the package date and the instructions on the label come first. It does not describe a food that has been left out, stored at the wrong temperature, or already cooked unless the product is listed that way (for example, there are separate entries for cooked and processed items). The data are American and from 2018; local conditions, such as a hot kitchen or an unreliable power supply, can change what is sensible, and this site gives no advice on that.

Using the pages

Open a food, look at the heading that matches your situation (opened or unopened, bought today or earlier), and read the tips. If the food is not in the data, this site has no number for it.

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service, FoodKeeper data (English JSON, data version 128, modified 6 September 2018), https://www.fsis.usda.gov/shared/data/EN/foodkeeper.json, listed on data.gov ("FSIS - FoodKeeper Data", dataset last updated 22 January 2025), read on 13 November 2026.

Are the times about quality or safety?

The data file does not say. This site reports the values and does not interpret them.

What if my food is not listed?

There is no time for it in this data; ask the manufacturer or your local food safety authority.
